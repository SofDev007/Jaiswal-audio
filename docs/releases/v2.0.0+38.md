JARX 2.0.0 (build 38)

Smart shuffle, songs that replay without data, and your own space for each account.

What's new
- Smart shuffle: tap shuffle on a playlist and pick Smart shuffle. Your playlist plays shuffled with a suggested song after every three. Tap + on a suggestion in Up Next to keep it in the playlist, or the minus to never see it again. Your playlist only changes when you tap +.
- Replays without data: songs you play all the way through are kept on your phone (up to 300 MB). Play one again and it uses no data. The songs you haven't played for longest make room for new ones.
- Switch account: each Google account gets its own music on the phone. Signing in on a friend's phone no longer mixes your music with theirs. If the music on a phone belongs to no account, JARX asks whether it's yours first. Settings › Account lists the accounts on the phone to switch between them, add one or remove one.
