JARX 2.0.0 (build 37)

Your queue now plays in the right order.

What's new
- Add to queue: songs you queue play next, in the order you added them, before the rest of the playlist and before suggested songs. This works with shuffle too.
- Suggested songs arrive sooner: while you're listening to suggestions, the next five are added one song early, so there's no wait between them.
- The next song starts faster after you queue something or suggestions are added, because JARX gets it ready in advance.
- Required updates: if an update is ever required, JARX pauses the music and shows you, step by step, how to install it from the bell.
