JARX 2.0.0 (build 41)

Music videos in up to 1080p HD.

What's new
- Play video now goes up to 1080p HD, for the whole video. Update the YouTube Video plugin to version 6 first (Settings › Plugins).
- Video quality: Auto (the best your connection allows, up to 1080p), or pick 1080p, 720p, 480p, 360p, 240p or 144p. Each shows about how much data an hour uses, and JARX remembers your choice.
- Smoother video: it stays in time with the song by playing a touch faster or slower instead of jumping, so it no longer stutters, even on older phones.
- The video only plays while the player is open. It pauses behind the mini player, on other screens and when JARX is in the background, so it doesn't use data or battery out of sight.
- The screen stays on while a video plays.
- Full screen has controls: tap the video to show them. A seek bar, play and pause, previous and next song, and the settings button.
- Captions in full screen, in the video's own languages (English, हिन्दी and more). Turn them on or off with the CC button, and pick the language and the text size (Small, Medium or Large) in settings. JARX remembers your choices.
- Full screen carries on into the next song's video, and goes back to the player if the next song has no video.
- Library › Create playlist: the text is now a normal size for a phone screen.
- Lyrics › Adjust delay now works and is easier: tap a line the moment you hear it start and JARX lines the lyrics up exactly, however far off they were. Earlier / Later fine-tune it 0.1 s at a time (hold to keep going), and it says plainly where you are ("Lyrics 1.2 s later"). Your timing is saved when you tap Done, go back, or the song changes.
- Playlists: Download comes first and Shuffle second, so the Shuffle / Smart shuffle choices fit on the screen. Back now closes those choices instead of leaving them on screen.
