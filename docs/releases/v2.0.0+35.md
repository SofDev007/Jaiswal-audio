JARX 2.0.0 (build 35)

Your music now follows your Google account.

What's new
- Sign in with Google. Your playlists, Liked songs, your 50 latest plays and what JARX learned about your taste come back on any phone you sign in on.
- Two phones stay in sync. Make, change or delete a playlist on one phone and the other phone gets it a few seconds later (or the next time JARX opens).
- Your Google photo on Settings › Account, with Sync now, Sign out and Delete my account.
- New accounts are approved by the owner before they can play.
- Update notices: new versions show up in the Home bell with the full list of changes and a Download button.
- Search: tapping a suggestion keeps you on All.
- Bottom bar: Search opens with the keyboard ready; Home, Library, Local and Offline jump back to the top (Library also closes an open playlist).
- Playlists: a search button sits next to Download; Playlist info moved into the ⋮ menu.
- JARX now counts your plays and listening time per song and artist, for the upcoming "Your corner".
- Safer: no plain http traffic, no phone backups of JARX's data, and plugins are checked against a fingerprint before they're installed.

Good to know
- Downloaded songs stay on the phone that downloaded them. On another phone the playlist is there and streams; tap Download all to keep it offline.
- JARX works offline for up to 10 days, then needs the internet once to confirm your account.
- Privacy policy: https://jarx.jarx-backend.workers.dev/privacy

Install: download jarx_android_v2.0.0+35_arm64-v8a.apk below and open it. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps.
