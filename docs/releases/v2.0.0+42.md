JARX 2.0.0 (build 42)

Your corner, a friendlier first start, and smarter downloads.

What's new
- Your corner: tap your photo at the top of Home. See how long you've listened this month, your top 5 artists and songs, and a silly comparison (how many Titanic movies is that?). Your photo took the timer's place; the sleep timer is still in the player.
- Downloads: a whole playlist now shows as one card in Offline, "27 of 59 downloaded", with what the rest are doing. Tap it to see its songs. New "Cancel all" button next to Pause all.
- Fixed: songs that were waiting to download when JARX closed stayed "Queued" forever. They now carry on by themselves.
- Fixed: cancelling one song of a playlist download no longer leaves the "playlist downloaded" message waiting forever.
- Fixed: the Shuffle button on playlists did nothing (build 41).
- Faster sync between your phones: while JARX is open it checks every minute (and when you come back to it), so a song you add on one phone shows up on the other within a minute. It costs almost no data or battery.
- New to JARX? A quick start: pick your languages and a few favourite artists, choose how Home looks, and bring your playlists from Spotify. Recommendations start from your picks.
- Small tips show the first time a feature matters, once each and never more than 3 a day.
- Funnier empty screens and loading lines.
- About JARX: a "More about the Developer" section.
