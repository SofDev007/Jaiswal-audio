JARX 2.0.0 (build 39)

Watch the music video while you listen.

What's new
- Play video: on the player screen, songs from YouTube have a Play video button just above the song name. Tap it and the cover turns into the song's music video, in time with the song. It plays inside JARX, without sound of its own: what you hear is still JARX, with your equalizer.
- Video quality and full screen: the buttons on the video pick the quality and fill the screen sideways. Back returns to the player.
- Videos play only while the player screen is open, so they use data only while you're watching. Tap Show cover to go back to the cover.
- Needs the YouTube Video plugin (Settings › Plugins).
